# Trademarks

The project name **daimon** and the project's logos are trademarks of the project owner(s).

## What is allowed

You may:

- refer to the project as "daimon" when describing unmodified or modified versions of the software;
- state that your fork is "based on daimon" or "forked from daimon";
- use the word mark "daimon" in nominative ways to truthfully describe compatibility.

## What is NOT allowed (without explicit permission)

You may not:

- distribute modified versions under the name "daimon" in a way that implies your build is an official build;
- use the daimon logos or branding in your fork/product name, website, or marketing materials;
- register domains, social accounts, or package names that impersonate the official project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome, but they must:

- use a distinct name and branding for redistributed builds;
- clearly indicate they are unofficial and maintained independently.
